Calculator history

Jack Kilby and the path to handheld calculation.

Jack St. Clair Kilby was an American electrical engineer who helped create the first integrated circuit while working at Texas Instruments in 1958. He also co-invented the handheld calculator and received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2000.

Why this matters

Calculators became practical because integrated circuits made electronic devices smaller, faster, and more reliable. A simple calculator project is a useful way to practice the same idea in software: break work into clear parts, connect them carefully, and make the result easy to use.
